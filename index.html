Spatial Selection Bias in Broadband Data

            MSc thesis
          

          

            Ookla + RURA data→
            Observation model→
            Cross-fit calibration→
            Corrected estimates
          

          
Compares Ookla's self-selected speed tests against RURA's full subscriber census, then models the probability a location enters the crowdsourced sample to correct for it. Thirty quarters of measurements, terrain, and technology mix pulled from 28 regulator PDFs feed an out-of-fold calibration step.
